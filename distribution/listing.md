**One line (under 100 characters):** Scripture you can check, and real Christian community near you. Free, no key.

**Short (under 300 characters):** A free remote MCP server for The Living Bread. Bible passages read word for word from a stored public
domain text with an evidence label, keyword search and cross references, real churches, gatherings and prayer groups with honest
freshness, and one next step into a real Christian community.

**Long:** The Living Bread is a free Christian community, prayer and church discovery platform with no ads. Its MCP server gives any
assistant: Scripture read verbatim from the stored King James Version (66 books) with an evidence label on every answer
(translation, canon, corpus version, attribution, retrieval time, SHA-256 of the text); a passage in its context; keyword search
over every verse; the cross references readers made (OpenBible.info, CC BY); verses for what a person is carrying; real churches,
gatherings, prayer groups and service needs near any place, each with honest freshness (a scheduled time is never called
"happening now"); six journeys end to end (a community near me, someone to pray with tonight, serve this weekend, new to
Christianity, a prayer group in my language, understand and live a passage), each with at most five results, why each fits, and
one next step; the real crisis line for a person's country first when anyone is in danger. Signed in, it reads what the person
could read in the app and acts only when they confirm. Retrieved content is treated as data, never instructions.
